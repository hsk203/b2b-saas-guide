Email marketing returns an average of $36 for every $1 spent, making it the highest-ROI marketing channel available to real estate professionals. Email converts 40% better than social media for lead generation. The average real estate email open rate is approximately 21%, with well-segmented campaigns doing significantly better.

But not every email platform is built for agents. You need listing updates, open house invites, sphere nurture sequences, and ideally a CRM that knows which contacts are hot. This guide compares the leading options for 2026, with real pricing from official sources. Prices change; verify on each vendor's site before you buy.

## 1. Mailchimp — best for design & branding

The most recognizable email platform in the world. Drag-and-drop editor, extensive template library, detailed analytics. Pre-built templates for property showcases, market updates, open house announcements, and newsletters.

- **Pricing:** free plan for small lists, then paid plans from about $13/month, scaling by contact tier.
- **Limitation:** pricing at scale. Mailchimp charges by contact count, and agents accumulate contacts over years — the bill grows even if half the list never opens.

## 2. Constant Contact — best for beginners

Simple email with phone support and event tools, aimed squarely at small local businesses. Real estate-friendly templates, event registration, and text messaging.

- **Pricing:** Lite from $12/month, Standard from $35/month, Premium from $80/month, scaling by contact tier.
- **Best for:** agents who want simple email, event registration, and phone support without a learning curve.

## 3. ActiveCampaign — best for advanced automation

The deepest automation on this list. Stage-based nurture sequences that respond to behavior: a contact who views a listing page triggers one sequence; a contact who opens a market report triggers another; a lead score crossing a threshold triggers a high-priority alert to your phone.

- **Pricing:** Starter from $15/mo at 1,000 contacts on annual billing; Plus from $49/mo, around $239/mo at 10,000 contacts.
- **Best for:** teams that want stage-based nurture and lead scoring without buying a full agent platform.

## 4. MailerLite — best value for small lists

The cheapest capable option for a clean monthly newsletter and simple nurture. MailerLite renamed its plans in June 2026: Comfort replaced Growing Business.

- **Pricing:** free up to 250 subscribers and 2,500 monthly emails; Comfort from $12/month (at 1,000 subscribers, $19/mo monthly billing), scaling by subscriber band. Includes 50 automations with up to 100 steps, 10 landing pages, 10 forms, unlimited templates, 3 seats.
- **Best for:** solo agents with 500–3,000 contacts who want a newsletter and a nurture sequence on one low monthly bill.

## 5. HubSpot — best for marketing analytics & attribution

HubSpot's Marketing Hub combines email, CRM, landing pages, and analytics in one platform, with the strongest attribution reporting on this list.

- **Pricing:** free tools include email marketing with 2,000 sends per calendar month (with HubSpot branding) and 1 automated action. Marketing Hub Professional sits at a noticeably higher price point, reflecting its enterprise-oriented positioning.
- **Best for:** larger brokerages and marketing teams that need sophisticated CRM, marketing analytics, automation, and attribution.

## 6. Follow Up Boss — best for lead routing & follow-up

The CRM most high-producing US agents and teams run their pipeline on. Less an email tool, more a lead management system with email built in.

- **Best for:** producing agents and teams running paid lead generation, who need CRM-driven lead routing across multiple sources.

## 7. Kit — best for a large sphere on a free plan

Kit's free plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers with unlimited landing pages, forms, and email broadcasts. The catch: the free plan has no visual automations and no automated sequences, so a lead nurture sequence needs the paid Creator plan ($390 billed yearly at 1,000 subscribers).

## How to choose

- **Solo agent, small list (< 1,000 contacts), just getting started:** MailerLite (free) or Constant Contact ($12/mo). Keep it simple.
- **Growing list (1,000–5,000 contacts), want automation:** ActiveCampaign Starter ($15/mo) or MailerLite Comfort. ActiveCampaign wins if you want behavioral triggers; MailerLite wins on price.
- **Team or brokerage, need CRM + attribution:** HubSpot or Follow Up Boss. HubSpot for analytics; Follow Up Boss for lead routing.
- **Luxury / brand-forward positioning:** Flodesk ($19–$54/mo) — design-first email where presentation is the differentiator.
- **Large sphere, tight budget:** Kit's free plan (10,000 subscribers) if you can live without automations for now.

## The six drip campaigns every agent needs

Regardless of platform, every real estate agent needs these six automated sequences: new lead welcome, listing alerts, open house follow-up, past-client nurture, sphere newsletter, and expired-listing re-engagement. Set these up once and they run for years.
